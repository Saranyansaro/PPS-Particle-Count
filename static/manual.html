PPS Particle Count Report – Manual

Install on iPhone or laptop, enter tests, find records and keep your data safe

0. iPhone app (no laptop needed)

The app is also published on the internet as an installable app. It then works on the iPhone on its own, even with no signal at the plant.

  1. On the iPhone, open the app's web address in Safari (it must be Safari, not Chrome or WhatsApp's browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the new PPS Report icon. It opens full screen like any other app.
Where the iPhone keeps your records: on the iPhone itself, inside the app. Nothing is sent to the internet. The Records screen says Saved on this device only.
Always open it from the home-screen icon. Safari tabs and the home-screen app keep separate records, and iPhone may clear records of websites you don't visit for a few weeks. The home-screen app is not cleared that way.

PDF on the iPhone

Tap Share PDF. The iPhone share sheet opens: send it on WhatsApp or Mail, or choose Save to Files. If a small box says Your PDF is ready, tap Share / Save (the iPhone sometimes wants a second tap).

Back up the iPhone every week

Records → Backup file → Save to Files (iCloud Drive or Google Drive), or send it to yourself on WhatsApp or email. A red dot on Records reminds you until your first backup, then again 7 days after the last one. If the phone is lost, the backup file is the only copy.

Updates

When a new version is published, a blue bar says A new version of the app is ready. Tap Update now. Your records are not touched.

1. One-time setup (laptop)

  1. Install Python (free) from python.org/downloads. Version 3.9 or newer.
    On Windows, on the first install screen, tick "Add python.exe to PATH" before clicking Install. This is the step most people miss.
  2. Unzip the PPS_Particle_Count_App folder somewhere permanent, for example Documents\PPS_Particle_Count_App. Don't run it from inside the zip or from the Downloads folder you clean out.
  3. That's all. No other software, no internet needed.

2. Starting the app

Easiest: double-click

  • Windows: double-click start.bat.
  • Mac: double-click start.command (first time: right-click → Open).
  • Phone on the same Wi-Fi: start_lan.bat (Windows) or start_lan.command (Mac).

A black window opens and your browser opens http://localhost:8765 with the app. Keep the black window open while you work. Closing it stops the app; your data is already saved.

From Visual Studio Code

  1. File → Open Folder → choose PPS_Particle_Count_App.
  2. Terminal → New Terminal.
  3. Type python server.py and press Enter (on Mac: python3 server.py).
  4. The browser opens by itself. If it doesn't, open http://localhost:8765.
  5. To stop: click in the terminal and press Ctrl+C.
Tip: bookmark http://localhost:8765 in your browser. It only works while the app is running.

3. Making a report

  1. Click New report. The report number fills itself (PPS/PC/26-27/001, 002 …). It restarts at 001 each financial year.
  2. Report: check the dates and type your name in Tested by. Your name and the counter details are remembered for every future report.
  3. 01 Client & sample: type the client name first. The report starts saving automatically as soon as it has a client name. The top bar shows Saved ✓.
  4. 02 Instrument: set once. Choose Hazy or Free water if the sample looked that way; the tool then recommends moisture removal.
  5. 03 Results: type the counts per ml from the counter slip (≥4, ≥6, ≥14, ≥21) and the NAS class. ISO codes calculate themselves and should match the slip.
    • Free test (before only): for a first visit.
    • After PPS filtration job: also enter the counts measured after purification. The report shows the improvement.
  6. 04 Target: pick the machine's most critical component. If the OEM manual gives a figure, type it over.
  7. 05 Recommendation: ticks itself from the results. Change any box if you disagree. Click Write observations from the results for a ready paragraph, then edit it.
  8. Plant & follow-up: contact, decision maker, power packs, oil spend, status, next step. None of this prints on the client's report.
  9. Click Save PDF (on a phone: Share PDF). The PDF goes to your Downloads folder, named with client and report number.

The right side shows the report exactly as it will print. On a phone, use the Enter / Report / Records switch at the top.

If the app or phone closes while you type, nothing is lost: the next time you open it, it says Unsaved changes were recovered. A red note under Report no. means another saved report already has that number.

4. Records list

Click Records in the top bar (the number shows how many are saved).

  • Search by client, report number, equipment, contact, industry, address or phone.
  • Sort with the drop-down, or click a column heading (Report no., Test date, Client, Status, Next step). Click again to reverse.
  • Filter by status with the buttons: Sample taken, Report delivered, Quote sent, Won, Lost. Each shows its count.
  • A red next-step date means it's overdue and the lead isn't closed.
  • Click any row (or Open) to open that report and edit it. Changes save automatically.
  • Delete needs two taps: Delete, then Confirm delete. Tap Undo in the message at the bottom within 10 seconds to bring it back; after that, only a backup can.
  • Export to Excel (CSV) gives you every record with all plant details in one sheet.
  • Backup file saves every record in one file. Restore / import reads such a file back (see section 6).
  • Press Esc or Back to report to return.

5. Where data is saved and backups

WhatWhere
All records (the database)data\pps_records.db inside the app folder
Automatic backupdata\backups\: one copy per day the app runs, the last 60 are kept
Backup on demandRecords → Backup file (works everywhere), or Database copy (.db) on the laptop
Before every importThe laptop saves a copy in data\backups\ named …_before_import.db
iPhone appOn the phone only. Back up with Records → Backup file every week
Excel copyRecords → Export to Excel (CSV)
Every Friday: copy the whole data folder to Google Drive or a pen drive. The automatic backups sit on the same computer; if the laptop is lost or the disk fails, only an outside copy saves you.
Never delete the data folder. When you get a new version of the app, copy your old data folder into the new app folder.

Restoring from a backup

Easiest, while the app keeps running: Records → Restore / import and choose a PPS_backup_….json file. Records are merged; nothing is deleted.

Putting a whole database copy back (a .db file from data\backups):

  1. Stop the app (close the black window).
  2. In the app folder, open a terminal (in VS Code: Terminal → New Terminal) and type, with the file you want:
    python server.py --restore data\backups\pps_records_2026-09-25.db
    (Mac: python3 and / instead of \)
  3. It says how many records it restored. The database you had before is kept in data\backups as …_before_restore.db.
  4. Start the app again.

Moving to another computer

Copy the whole app folder (including data), install Python on the new computer, and start as usual.

6. Phone and laptop together

Option A: the phone uses the laptop's database (same Wi-Fi)

Works only while the laptop is on and running the app, and both are on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. Start with start_lan.bat (Mac: start_lan.command, or python server.py --lan).
  2. The black window shows a link like http://192.168.1.25:8765/?key=Xy7…. Open that exact link on your phone (the ?key= part is the password that stops other people on the Wi-Fi from seeing your records). The phone remembers it.
  3. If Windows Firewall asks, allow access on Private networks only.

If start.bat is already open, close it first: start_lan tells you so. To change the key (for example after a phone is lost), stop the app, delete data\lan_key.txt and start start_lan again; open the new link on your phones.

On this address, Share PDF may download the file instead of opening the share sheet; that's normal.

Option B: the iPhone app on its own, then move records to the laptop

  1. On the iPhone: Records → Backup file → send it to yourself (WhatsApp Web, email, AirDrop, Google Drive).
  2. On the laptop: Records → Restore / import → choose that file.
  3. New records are added. A record that exists on both is kept in whichever copy was edited last. Nothing is deleted.

It works the other way too (laptop backup → import on the phone).

Report numbers are counted on each device separately. If you make reports on both the phone and the laptop in the same week, import first, then start new reports, so the numbers don't repeat. The app warns you when a number is already used.

7. What the numbers mean

  • Before / After: Before is the oil as you found it. After is the same machine re-tested once PPS filtration is done. For a free test, fill Before only.
  • Counts are cumulative. The ≥14 µm count includes every particle 14 µm and bigger, so it already contains the ≥21 µm particles. The ≥21 count can never be higher than ≥14. If the app warns about this, you mistyped.
  • ISO 4406 code (e.g. 21/20/19): three numbers for ≥4, ≥6 and ≥14 µm. Each step up roughly doubles the particle count.
  • NAS 1638 class: taken straight from the counter slip. 12 is the dirtiest class on the scale.
  • "31× the allowed particles": the measured count divided by the most the target allows, at the worst particle size. This is the headline for the client.
  • Target NAS is an approximate conversion from the ISO target. Follow the OEM manual where you have it.

8. Problems and fixes

"python is not recognized" or start.bat says Python is not installedInstall Python again and tick Add python.exe to PATH. Restart the computer.
Red bar: "Can't reach the app server"The black window was closed. Start the app again. Keep the page open: what you typed saves by itself when the server is back (it also survives a reload).
Red bar: "This phone needs the access link"Open the full link shown in the laptop's black window, the one ending in ?key=….
Top bar says "Not saved"Same as above: the app server isn't running, or see the orange bar.
Orange bar: "This report was changed in another window or on another device"Someone saved the same report elsewhere after you opened it. Show the newer copy drops your unsaved changes; Keep my version saves yours over theirs.
"Replace what you typed?" under ObservationsTap again to replace your text with the written paragraph, or leave it. After replacing, Undo brings your text back.
Deleted a record by mistakeTap Undo in the message at the bottom (10 seconds). Later: restore a backup.
"The app is already running"It is open in another black window; the browser opens it for you. Use that one.
"Port 8765 is used by another program"Close that program, or start with python server.py --port 8766 and open http://localhost:8766.
Page is blank or old-lookingPress Ctrl+F5 to reload.
"Add client name to save"Reports save only once they have a client name.
PDF didn't appearLook in your Downloads folder. If your browser blocks downloads, allow them for localhost.
